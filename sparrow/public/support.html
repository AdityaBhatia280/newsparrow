Support

How can we help?

Google says my school account is blocked

Some schools only allow apps they’ve approved on student Google accounts. Sign in with a personal Google account instead, or ask your school’s IT team to allow Sparrow.

Why do I sign in with publik too?

Sparrow’s AI features run on publik under your own account, so your AI usage is yours. Signing in with publik once connects it. Sparrow never sees your publik password.

The advisor says I’m out of allowance

AI requests use your publik allowance. Use the link in the message to get a plan on publik, or add your own publik API key in Settings. Everything else in Sparrow works without AI.

A listing has the wrong information

Open it in Explore and choose “Suggest a correction”, then add the official page that shows the right details. A reviewer checks every correction.

How do I download or delete my data?

Go to Settings. “Export my data” downloads everything as a file. “Delete account” permanently deletes your Sparrow data.

Sparrow is slow on my school laptop

Open Settings → Appearance and choose Reduced. It turns off the glass blur and animations.
