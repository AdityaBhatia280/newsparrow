Privacy Policy · Last updated November 24, 2026

Your privacy

Sparrow is a planning app for high school students. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run Sparrow, and the choices you have.

Who can use Sparrow

Sparrow is for students 13 and older. If you are under 18, use Sparrow with a parent or guardian’s permission. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your first name from your Google profile.
  • What you tell us: your survey answers (grade, state, interests, goals, time and budget), activities and honors, skills, saved opportunities with their notes, dates and tasks, your college shortlist and notes, advisor conversations, and opportunities you suggest.
  • Technical: essential cookies that keep you signed in, and basic server logs needed to keep Sparrow secure and working.

Resumes: when you import a resume, the file is read inside your browser and is never uploaded to Sparrow. Emails, phone numbers, links and addresses are removed from the text before you see it. Only text you review and choose to send for analysis leaves your device, and Sparrow does not store that text. We save only the activities you choose to keep.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your plan on any device.
  • To match you with opportunities and colleges based on your answers.
  • To answer AI requests, only when you confirm them.
  • To review suggested opportunities and award reward points.
  • To keep Sparrow safe, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your information to train AI models.

AI features and publik

Sparrow’s AI features run through publik, using your own publik account. Before any AI request, Sparrow shows what will be sent and asks you to confirm. We never send your name, email, account ID or contact details to AI. publik also receives content-free usage pings (“app opened” and “still here”) so it can count Sparrow’s users; you can turn these off in Settings. How publik handles AI requests is described in publik’s privacy policy.

Who helps us run Sparrow

  • Neon: our database.
  • Vercel: hosting for the website and server.
  • publik: AI features and usage counting, as described above.
  • Google: sign-in. Google tells Sparrow your name, email address and that the email is verified; Sparrow never sees your Google password.

These providers process information only to provide their service to Sparrow. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and storage

We use only essential cookies, for signing in and keeping you signed in. Your browser also stores a few preferences, such as visual effects and an unsent advisor draft. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping it safe

Information travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in cookies are encrypted and can’t be read by page scripts. Keys for AI services are encrypted on our servers and never sent to your browser. Only Sparrow’s server can access the database.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and activities at any time.
  • Download everything Sparrow stores about you from Settings → Export my data.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete account. This removes your information from our live database right away. Copies in our providers’ routine backups are deleted on their regular schedules.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and let you know in the app.
