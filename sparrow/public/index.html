How it works

Three steps to your plan

  1. 01

    Tell us about you

    A two-minute survey about your grade, interests, time and goals. Drop in a resume and Sparrow pulls out your activities. The file never leaves your device.

  2. 02

    Get matched

    See extracurriculars, competitions, internships and scholarships ranked by fit, plus colleges worth a look, each with a reason why.

  3. 03

    Make it happen

    Track every application on a board, check off tasks, export dates to your calendar, and ask the advisor when you're stuck.

Features

Everything in one place

Explore

Real opportunities, ranked for you

Programs, competitions, research, internships and scholarships, each linked to its official site. Filter by interest, grade, format and cost, or search the web with AI.

Computer scienceMatches meFreeVirtual

Colleges

1,700+ colleges, official data

Cost, size, graduation rates and top programs from the U.S. Department of Education. Shortlist, take notes and compare side by side.

Plan

Every application on one board

Drag from Interested to Submitted, add tasks and target dates, and export it all to your calendar.

Advisor

AI that sticks to the facts

Ask for a plan, a comparison or feedback on a draft. It only uses facts Sparrow can show you, labels its time estimates, and never writes your essays for you.

Activities list

Ready when applications are

Build a Common App-style list with live 50, 100 and 150-character counters, tighten descriptions with AI in your own voice, and print a brag sheet for recommenders.

Resume

Read on your device

Drop in a PDF or Word resume. Sparrow reads it in your browser, removes contact details, and turns it into activities you approve.

Privacy

Your data, your call

  • Never sold. No ads, no selling your information, ever.
  • Read on your device. Your resume file stays in your browser; you choose what text is shared.
  • AI only when you ask. Nothing is sent to AI without your confirmation, and never your name or contact details.
  • Yours to take or delete. Export everything or delete your account any time in Settings.
